Add retry with backoff to the webhook sender

Objective: retry failed webhook deliveries with exponential backoff so transient 5xx responses stop dropping events.

What to do:
- Add a retry policy with a maximum of 5 attempts and jittered backoff.
- Persist the attempt count so retries survive a restart.

Out of scope: do not change the webhook payload format or the signing logic.

How to validate: simulate 503 responses from a test endpoint and confirm delivery succeeds on the third attempt.
